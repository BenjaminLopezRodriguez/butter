# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Software teams reviewing an in-progress build. A developer records a narrated walkthrough; teammates need to address precise product concerns without losing the moment, the evidence, or the later decision.

## Product Purpose

Butter turns a recorded, narrated product demo into timestamped findings that teams review with the discipline of a pull request. It gathers evidence and proposes concerns; people decide whether to accept, discuss, dismiss, resolve, or investigate them.

## Brand Personality

Exacting, restrained, candid. It should feel like a trustworthy engineering artifact: calm under uncertainty, specific about what it saw, and unwilling to pretend correlation is causation.

## Anti-references

Avoid recorder and generic AI-tool language; dashboard theater; abstract 3D art; gradient meshes; glass cards; invented traction; vague feature claims; and marketing that obscures the working product. The UI is not a video gallery or a generic UX-critique feed.

## Design Principles

- Show the review itself before describing it.
- Make every concern individually addressable and evidence-led.
- Use diff semantics precisely: finding type is meaningful color, not decoration.
- Treat uncertainty as a useful result; name the evidence that would settle it.
- Keep the surrounding system quiet so a finding and its decision can carry attention.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain WCAG AA contrast for all text and controls, visible keyboard focus, semantic controls, readable touch targets on mobile, and a reduced-motion experience without dependent animation.
